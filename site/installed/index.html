Installed. One more step.

DragonSheets lives inside Google Sheets — there's nothing to open here.

  1. Open any Google Sheet — an existing one or a blank one.
  2. Click the green DragonSheets button in the Sheets toolbar (top left, next to the toolbar icons).
  3. Sign in with Google and follow the 2-minute setup — you'll connect Amazon and pick your first report.
